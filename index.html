Free 30-Day NCLEX-RN® Study Plan

For working nurses. One topic a day, no topic left out. Tick each day as you go: your ticks are saved on this phone.

How to use this plan

  1. Study twice a day: 45 minutes before work and 45-60 minutes after. Do the day's topic, then the questions.
  2. After every question, read the rationale for the right answer AND the wrong ones. Log each miss in one line.
  3. Missed a day? Do not start over. Do the next topic tomorrow. A short day (25 minutes + 20 questions) still counts.

The exam in numbers

Adaptive test: 85 to 150 items, up to 5 hours, including optional breaks (2026 NCSBN NCLEX-RN® Test Plan).

8 client-need areas: Management of Care 15-21%; Safety and Infection Prevention and Control 10-16%; Pharmacological and Parenteral Therapies 13-19%; Physiological Adaptation 11-17%; Reduction of Risk Potential 9-15%; Health Promotion and Maintenance 6-12%; Psychosocial Integrity 6-12%; Basic Care and Comfort 6-12%.

Next Generation items test clinical judgement: recognise cues, analyse cues, prioritise hypotheses, generate solutions, take action, evaluate outcomes.

Daily questions

Work day: about 40 questions (35 on heavy days). Day off: about 100 questions and 1 case study. Short day: 20 questions. It counts.

Week 1: Foundations

Tip: Write down your Day 1 score. It is your starting line, not a verdict.

Week 2: Body Systems

Checkpoint guide (our guide, not an NCSBN rule): 55% or more = on track. 45-54% = add 30 minutes a day. Under 45% = plan 6-8 weeks instead of 4.

Tip: In select-all questions, judge each option on its own as true or false.

Week 3: Special Groups

Tip: For "first" or "priority" questions, ask: which client or finding could cause harm soonest?

Week 4: Mental Health, Safety and Mocks

Checkpoint guide (our guide, not an NCSBN rule): 65-70% or more with no area under 55% = ready to book or keep your date. 60-64% = take Mock 3, then decide. Under 60% = add 1-2 weeks.

Mock pace: 5 hours for up to 150 items is about 2 minutes each. Aim for 1.5-2 minutes.

Week 5: Finish

Tip: Keep the last days light. Rest is part of the plan.
