Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026. This page covers this website, the free 30-day study plan. Right now the site collects no personal data.

Who is responsible

The Migrating Nurse, the person who runs this site and the Instagram account @migratingnurse.global, is responsible for any personal data collected here (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

Your ticks

When you tick a day, the tick is saved in your own browser's storage (local storage, under the name tmn-plan-v1) on your phone or computer, so your progress is still there next time. This site's code never sends your ticks anywhere. If you have turned on browser sync, your browser may copy site data to your browser account.

Other sites hosted at riya-projects.github.io share the same browser storage area. To delete your ticks, use the Clear my ticks button on the plan page.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party scripts, fonts or forms. Everything on the page is loaded from this site.
  • No account or log-in.

Hosting

GitHub, Inc. hosts this site on GitHub Pages. To deliver and protect the site, GitHub processes connection data such as your IP address, including in the United States. See GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use that data.

Email sign-up (not open yet)

The email form on the plan page is switched off and collects nothing. Before it opens, this page will be updated first. It will say which email service holds the list, what we send and how often, and give an email address for privacy requests. Your address would be used only to send emails about the study plan, and only if you tick the consent box yourself. Every email will have an unsubscribe link.

Under the DPDP Act you can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. You can also ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, and name someone to act for you. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Questions

For now, send a direct message to @migratingnurse.global on Instagram. Instagram is run by Meta, which processes those messages.
